Legal & regulatory consultant

James Bunn

I work with organisations in the cannabis and psychedelic sector, helping them navigate regulation, structure their operations, and communicate clearly in an industry still being defined. I work primarily through Psilonautica, a consultancy I founded, which focuses on supporting organisations in this space.

Before this, I worked within UK drug and alcohol treatment services, where the gap between what people actually needed help with and how the law classified it became hard to ignore. That observation still shapes the work I do now.
