Legal & regulatory consultant

James Bunn

James works with organisations in the cannabis and psychedelic sector, helping them navigate regulation, structure their operations, and communicate clearly in an industry still being defined. He works through Psilonautica, the consultancy he founded, to support other organisations in the space directly.

Before this, he worked within UK drug and alcohol treatment services, where the gap between what people actually needed help with and how the law classified it became hard to ignore. That observation still shapes the work he does now.
