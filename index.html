About

James Bunn helps organisations in the cannabis and psychedelic space run smoothly — managing operations, communications, and the messy bits in between. His background in law means he knows what the rules are and how best to navigate the regulations, while his hands-on experience covers everything from team leadership to public relations and digital marketing.

He previously worked in UK drug and alcohol services, where he saw first-hand the gap between the substances people actually sought treatment for — alcohol, tobacco, opiates, benzodiazepines, cocaine — and official drug classification frameworks. That experience shaped his ongoing research and advocacy work in cannabis and psychedelics.

James cares about work that matters: improving harm reduction efforts, making systems more efficient, and helping teams collaborate better.

Areas of focus

  • Regulatory & legal navigation

    Advising on law and regulatory pathways for psychedelic and cannabis organisations across the UK, Canada, Australia, USA, Israel, and various EU member states.

  • Operations

    Running the day-to-day of growing organisations — governance, HR, auditing, and accountancy.

  • Communications & PR

    Public relations and digital marketing for organisations working in emerging, heavily-regulated industries.

  • Harm reduction

    Practical, evidence-based approaches to reducing harm, informed by frontline experience in drug and alcohol services.
