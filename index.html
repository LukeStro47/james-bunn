Profile

A grounding in law, applied to an emerging industry

James Bunn works with organisations in the cannabis and psychedelic sector that need practical support navigating regulation, structuring operations, and communicating with confidence. His legal training gives him a clear read on what the rules actually require, while his operational background means he can translate that into decisions a growing organisation can act on: governance, human resources, auditing, accountancy, and day to day management.

Before moving into this sector, he worked within UK drug and alcohol treatment services. That role exposed a persistent gap: the substances people most commonly sought treatment for, among them alcohol, tobacco, opiates, benzodiazepines, and cocaine, sat oddly against the official classification frameworks meant to govern them. That observation set the direction for the research, advisory, and advocacy work he has pursued since.

His current focus sits at the intersection of harm reduction, regulatory pathway design, and organisational efficiency: helping teams operate more soundly while the frameworks around them are still being written.
